# Classy for teachers: the agreed plan

Agreed with the owner on 24–25 November 2026, question by question. Sections 1–9 are the owner's decisions. Section 10 lists smaller points nobody was asked about, with the default this plan uses. Section 11 lists what the owner chose against the recommendation, with the risk that follows.

Today teachers are not users. A teacher is only the name written on a section ("Raed Bolbol"). There are 91 such names: 83 people and 8 placeholder accounts such as "CS1 CS1".

## 1. Who can sign in, and how

- A teacher opens `/teach`, types their GUtech staff email (`firstname.lastname@gutech.edu.om`) and gets a 6-digit code there. Any address at exactly `@gutech.edu.om` is accepted. Student addresses (`@student.gutech.edu.om`) are not.
- The small "Staff? Sign in here" link on the student sign-in screen offers two choices: Teacher or Dashboard. That is the only place students see a teacher option.
- **Student teaching assistants:** an owner can give a student account teaching rights. That student gets a way into the teacher side from their menu.
- **Owners** can open the teacher view for any timetable name from the dashboard and try everything. Changes made this way are logged under the owner.
- A teacher stays signed in for 60 days. Changing a class asks for a new emailed code if their last code is more than 7 days old.
- Sign-in codes for teachers are never shown on screen, even in test mode.

## 2. Which classes are theirs

- **Name link.** If the email clearly matches exactly one timetable name (`raed.bolbol` → "Raed Bolbol"), the teacher is linked to it at once. Otherwise they pick their name from the list and an owner or RSA approves it. Placeholder names are not in the list. A "My name isn't here" button lets them carry on with an empty timetable.
- One name per teacher by default. Owners and RSA can attach more names to an account.
- **Adding sections.** On an "Edit my classes" page a teacher searches any course by code, name or teacher and ticks the sections they teach. Added sections show on their timetable at once.
- **Hiding sections.** A teacher can untick a section that is listed under their name but isn't theirs. It is hidden for them only.
- **Rights.** Every signed-in teacher has full rights at once on every section on their timetable, whether it came from their name or they added it, and whether or not their name has been approved yet. Approval decides only which name is theirs.
- A teacher who added a section is shown to students on that class straight away (office hours on the class page, the class on their teacher page).
- **New semester:** added sections start empty, with last semester's offered as suggestions to tick again.

## 3. The teacher's home

- The same day-and-week home students have (swipe for days, scroll down for the week), showing the classes they teach.
- **One real class, several codes.** A lecture listed under two or three course codes at the same time and room (67 of 306 sections) shows as one block. Class size is the combined total.
- Extras: add to my calendar (private link), reminders before class, own slots, and the free rooms page.
- Batch reps' published events for a teacher's sections show on the teacher's timetable (once the planner is switched on).

## 4. Changing a class

- A teacher can, for one future session at a time: **cancel it, move it** to another time or day, **change the room**, or **add an extra class**.
- Changes are live at once. No approval.
- For a class listed under several codes, the change applies to all of them in one tap; the teacher can untick a code before confirming.
- An optional short note goes with a change. Students see it on the class and in the notification.
- **Picking a time:** the six standard class slots are one-tap buttons, and any other time can be typed. Classy warns but never blocks: how many of the section's students have another class then, whether the room is taken, and when the time is outside 08:00–18:15, on a holiday, a Friday or Saturday, or outside the teaching weeks.
- **Picking a room:** from the rooms free at that time, or typed, or left "to be announced".
- **Late changes** are allowed until the class starts, with a warning ("This starts in 10 minutes; students may not see it in time").
- **Undo** is possible until the class starts.

## 5. What students see

- A cancelled class stays in its place, greyed out and marked **Cancelled**.
- A moved class shows at the new time with a **Moved** mark; the old slot stays greyed, saying "Moved to Tue 10:00".
- A room change and an extra class are marked too.
- Students always see who made the change ("Cancelled by Raed Bolbol") and the note.
- Students with change alerts on get a notification at once.
- Each class page shows the teacher's office hours and details. Tapping a teacher's name opens a **teacher page**: office hours, office room, note, email if the teacher turned it on, and the classes they teach this week.

## 6. Class size, office hours and the teacher's details

- **Class size:** the number of Classy students who picked the section, always the exact number, labelled as Classy users (it is not the official enrollment). When moving a class the teacher also sees how many of them have another class at the new time. Never names or IDs.
- **Office hours:** weekly times with a room. They show on the teacher's own timetable and to students.
- A teacher may also show students their office room, a short note, and their email (off unless they turn it on).
- Teachers do not see students' notes.

## 7. When the university timetable changes

- A teacher's change stays on top of the university timetable until they undo it.
- If the university later changes only the **room** of that class, the teacher's change stays. If a teacher changed the room and the university then lists another room, students keep seeing the teacher's room and the teacher is told.
- If the university moves that class to another day or time, or drops it, the teacher's change is dropped and the teacher is told.
- If the university gives a section to a different teacher, the first teacher's upcoming changes are kept; the new teacher and staff can undo them; both teachers are told.
- Teachers get change alerts, like students, when the university timetable changes one of their classes.

## 8. Safety

- When a teacher changes a class that is under another teacher's name, the named teacher is notified (if they use Classy) and can undo it.
- Owners and RSA see a **log of every teacher change** in the dashboard and can undo any of them. Changes made by someone who isn't the class's named teacher are flagged.
- Owners and RSA are alerted when a teacher makes more than 5 changes in a day. Nobody is stopped.
- **Emergency switches:** an owner-only switch turns off all teacher class changes at once; owners and RSA can freeze a single teacher.
- **Removing a teacher** blocks that email until an owner or RSA unblocks it. The remove dialog offers "also undo their N upcoming changes"; if ticked, students are told those classes are back on.

## 9. Dashboard and rollout

- Owners and RSA manage teachers: approve names, attach extra names, freeze, remove and unblock, and see and undo teacher changes.
- Everything ships together, live as soon as it is built and tested, this semester (about four teaching weeks are left; the last class is on 24 December).
- Launch does not wait for a test of email delivery to staff mailboxes.

## 10. Defaults for points nobody was asked about

Say so if any of these should be different.

**Accounts**
- One teacher account per timetable name. A matching email wins over someone who picked that name by hand; staff see the conflict.
- Each automatic name link shows in the dashboard with one tap to reverse it.
- Owners and RSA see how many teachers are waiting on the dashboard home. A waiting teacher sees the result the next time they open Classy.
- A teacher with no linked name is shown to students by the name in their email ("raed.bolbol" → "Raed Bolbol").
- If the university re-spells a teacher's name, the teacher keeps their account, office hours and added sections; staff get a one-tap "same person" button.
- "Delete all data" deletes teacher accounts and their changes. Blocked emails stay blocked.

**Changes**
- A cancelled class sends no reminder, doesn't count as a clash or as a missable class in the absence counter, and frees its room on the free rooms page. A moved or extra class takes its room at the new time.
- In phone calendars a cancelled class stays with "Cancelled:" at the start of its title; a moved class shows at its new time.
- A teacher can edit a change without undoing it first; students get one notification with the new details.
- Undoing a change tells the same students the class is back on.
- For a moved class, undo is possible until the earlier of the old and the new start time.
- When two teachers are on one section, either can undo or replace a change, and the other is told.
- When a section has two sessions on the same day, the teacher changes one, with a tick box "also the other session that day".
- An extra class is for the sections the teacher ticks; the teacher picks lecture, lab or tutorial; students can mark it missed like any class.
- The clash count looks at students' classes only, not their private own slots.
- A teacher's change wins over a batch rep's change to the same class.
- If the university timetable later shows exactly what the teacher did, the change merges quietly.
- Before staff apply a sync or upload, they see which teacher changes it will drop. A hand edit by RSA follows the same rules as a sync.
- When staff undo a teacher's change, the teacher is told.

**Pages**
- A teacher who has never signed in still has a teacher page with their classes this week. Placeholder names can't be tapped.
- Office hours skip holidays and the weeks outside teaching. A single week can't be cancelled; the teacher's note can say "away this week".
- RSA can see teachers' emails and the change log. They still see nothing about students.

## 11. Choices made against the recommendation

- **Any GUtech email can change any class.** Full rights at once on added sections, even before a name is approved, means anyone who receives mail at `@gutech.edu.om` (teachers, registrars, IT, assistants) can cancel or move any class in the university. What stands in the way of misuse: the named teacher is notified, the change shows who made it, staff see it flagged in the log and can undo it, staff are alerted past 5 changes a day, and the two emergency switches.
- **Exact numbers for small sections.** In a section with one or two Classy students, "1 of 1 has another class at 10:00" describes one identifiable student. Tried time after time, it could reveal that student's week.
- **No daily stop.** Staff are alerted, but a teacher can keep making changes.
- **No email test before launch.** Delivery to staff mailboxes has never been checked and it is the teachers' only way in. If GUtech's mail system filters the message, no teacher can sign in until that is fixed. The owner's "view as any teacher" still works.
- **Student TAs.** Giving a student account teaching rights means a student sign-in can change classes.

## Not in this update

- Messaging sections.
- Teachers reading students' notes.
- Cancelling one week of office hours.
- A page about this in the Updates PDF.
