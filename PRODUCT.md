# Classy

Classy is a phone-first GUtech timetable for students, with a separate staff dashboard. Students sign in with university IDs and codes, pick courses and sections, and browse dated classes, their own slots, course notes and shared groups.

## Semester Planner

Owner-appointed batch representatives collaborate in one workspace per major family and semester. They can include courses from any major. Batches are editable course/section selections; student event audiences follow enrollment, not major or year labels.

The planner combines calendar browsing, manual proposals, deterministic free-slot searches and AI message interpretation. Imported university schedules are preserved. Drafts are private to workspace collaborators; a rep explicitly publishes a revision before students see it. Warnings require acknowledgment and an explanation. All dates and scheduling decisions use Oman time.

The planner extends the established cream, navy, sky and ice interface. Preserve existing student swipe/snap navigation. Desktop planning can be denser than student pages; mobile retains all actions through explicit controls, including an alternative to dragging.

The production planner is initially disabled. Owners can preview it, assign reps, and enable it after validation. AI uses the existing private Codex gateway and never publishes automatically.
